Yunho Choi

I am a final year PhD Student at Robot Learning Laboratory in Seoul National University (SNU), Korea. I am advised by Songhwai Oh . Previously, I obtained my bachelor's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering with highest honors from SNU in 2017.

My research focuses on robot learning, especially on decision making of embodied agents and reinforcement learning. I also work on visual localization of mobile robots with Sequor Robotics, Inc.
